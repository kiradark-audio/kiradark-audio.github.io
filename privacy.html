Privacy Policy - youtube-fleet

Last updated: November 18, 2026

What this app is

youtube-fleet is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

The app requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses it only to upload videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the YouTube channel the owner signed in with. It does not read, collect or store any other Google user data, and it does not access other people's data.

How data is stored

The OAuth token that Google issues is stored only on the owner's own machines and private infrastructure. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions. Revoking it deletes the app's ability to upload; the app keeps no copy of any data.
